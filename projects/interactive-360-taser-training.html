Interactive 360 TASER Training

Pitched, funded ($750K), directed, produced

Immersive police training scenarios that put the trainee inside real 360° footage, with interaction layered on in Unity. I saw this project through every stage, from pitch to deployment. Once we secured $750K in funding, I directed and produced a proof of concept that became a product deployed to more than 1,000 agencies. That meant planning each scene from overhead blocking diagrams, directing on set during shoots in Poland and Los Angeles, and running the final tech review and iteration in Unity.
